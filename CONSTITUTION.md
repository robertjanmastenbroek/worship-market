## 1. The Vision

To build the **Digital Sanctuary for Kingdom Creatives**.

WorshipMarket is not just a store; it is the premier ecosystem connecting **Partners** (creatives, producers, editors) with **Leaders** (pastors, worship leaders, media directors).

- **The Problem:** The current market is fragmented (Fiverr is secular/unsafe; BeatStars is just for rappers; MultiTracks is corporate/closed).
- **The Solution:** A "Theologically Safe" hybrid marketplace for Services, Digital Assets, and AI Knowledge.
- **The Tagline:** WorshipMarket: The Creative Economy for the Church.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy & Differentiators

### A. "Theological Safety" (The Moat)

In a world of deepfakes and heresy, we are the **Walled Garden**.

- **Human-Verified:** We do not rely solely on algorithms. Every seller agrees to a Statement of Faith.
- **Community Policing:** Strict "Report" systems empower the community to flag spiritually unsafe content.

### B. The AI Stance: "AI-Enabled, Human-Curated"

We do not fight the future; we steward it.

- **We Sell "Recipes" (Prompts):** We acknowledge that knowing how to use AI is a valuable skill.
- **We Sell "Curation" (Assets):** We allow AI-generated content (graphics/loops) ONLY IF it is human-curated, bug-fixed, and labeled "AI-Assisted."
- **The Red Line:** We prohibit "AI-Written Sermons." Technology can build the slide, but the Holy Spirit must write the message.

### C. The "Services First" Strategy

We avoid the "Empty Shelf" problem by launching with Services (which require no inventory) alongside the Founder's own high-quality assets (Electronic Worship).

## 4. The Roadmap: Phasing Strategy

### 🚩 Phase 1: The MVP (Foundation)

**Goal:** Validate transaction flow and onboard the "Founding 50" Partners.

**Inventory:**
- **Services:** Launch with The Sunday Team offering specific gigs (e.g., "Sermon Reels Editing").
- **Assets:** Launch with Electronic Worship Pads & Stems (The Flagship Product).

**Features:**
- User Roles: Partner vs. Leader.
- Search & Filter (Key, BPM, Vibe).
- Simple Checkout (Stripe Connect).
- "Request Board": Leaders post needs; Partners reply.

### 🚩 Phase 2: The Expansion (Visuals & Prompts)

**Goal:** Capture the "Pastor's Wallet" (Visuals are higher value than audio).

**New Categories:** Sermon Series Kits, Motion Backgrounds, AI Prompt Packs.

**Features:**
- Waveform Player: Instant visual audio preview.
- Collections: "The Easter Bundle 2026."

### 🚩 Phase 3: The Ecosystem (Scale)

**Goal:** Recurring Revenue & Automation.

**Features:**
- WorshipMarket Pro: Subscription for Partners (0% fees).
- Vector Search: "Find me a sound like this" (AI Search).
- The First Fruits: Auto-donate platform profits to charity.

## 6. Financial Model

- **Commission:** 15% Platform Fee (Competitive vs. 20-30% industry standard).
- **Minimum Fee:** $0.50 floor per transaction (Protects against micro-transaction losses).
- **Payouts:** Automated "Split Payments" via Stripe Connect (We never touch the seller's share).
- **Refunds:**
  - Services: Discretionary based on dispute resolution.
  - Assets/Prompts: Non-Refundable (Digital Goods policy) unless file is corrupt.

## 8. Guardrails & Ethics

- **Originality:** STRICTLY original content only. No covers of CCLI songs (e.g., "Oceans" backing tracks) in the MVP to avoid mechanical licensing liabilities.
- **Verification:** Sellers must self-certify: "I confirm this is my original work and aligns with the Apostles' Creed."
- **No "Spirit-Botting":** AI is a tool, not a replacement for the anointing. We prioritize human creativity assisted by technology.
